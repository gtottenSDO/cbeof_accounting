# Separations vs hiring: what drove the 2023–25 young-worker decline

Pulled Nov 2, 2026. Colorado, private sector.

## Bottom line

- The decline came from fewer hires, not more separations.
- Separation rates for workers under 35 were flat or lower.
- For 25–34-year-olds in NAICS 5412, firm job gains within that age group fell sharply while all-age gains held steady. Firm job losses rose, but by about as much as for the industry overall.
- LEHD has no direct quit-versus-layoff field. JOLTS does, but it has no state × detailed industry × age breakdown.

**How firm job gains and losses work (QWI):** each quarter, for each employer, end-of-quarter minus start-of-quarter employment is computed within the age group. Positive differences are summed as gains, negative ones as losses. These are net headcount changes, so "losses" include leavers who weren't replaced, not only layoffs.
