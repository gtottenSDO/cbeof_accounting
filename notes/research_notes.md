## Accounting trends (subagent research; verify before citing)

**Pipeline**
- US accounting bachelor's degrees fell 10.3% (2022–23), then 3.3% (2023–24).
- NASBA CPA exam candidates: 74,165 in 2024, 87,660 in 2025. New candidates: 27,994, then 36,590.
- The "75% of CPAs near retirement" figure comes from a 2015 AICPA estimate about members eligible by 2020. It is stale; don't cite it.

**Colorado SB26-076**
- Adds three pathways, including a bachelor's plus 2 years of experience. Effective Jan 1, 2027. Signed May 4, 2026 (per COCPA).
- Board rules were expected Oct 21, 2026. Final rule text is unverified.

**Projections and postings**
- Colorado 2024–34 (Projections Central via O*NET; not checked against CDLE directly): accountants +18% (3,720 openings a year), bookkeeping clerks +5%, tax preparers +28%.
- US 2025–35: accountants +5%, bookkeeping clerks −6%.
- Indeed accounting postings index: about 126 in Jan 2025, about 92 in summer 2026, 102.5 in Sep 2026 (Feb 2020 = 100).

**AI**
- Stanford "Canaries in the Coal Mine" paper (Brynjolfsson, Chandar and Chen, Aug 2025): 13% relative employment drop for workers aged 22–25 in AI-exposed jobs, accountants included.
- BambooHR survey: senior-to-entry hiring ratio of 3:1 in accounting and finance.
- PwC is cutting entry-level hiring.

**Private equity**
- About 24 of the Top 100 firms are PE-backed (CFO Brew, Apr 30, 2026). Top 100 mergers: 225 in 2025, up from 122.
- Colorado: Richey May (F3, then a Genstar/Aksia investment in Sep 2026; No. 50 on the Top 100). Aprio bought Denver's TaxOps, Pontiff and Elite Tax.

**Tax side (OBBBA)**
- New deductions for tips, overtime, seniors and car loan interest (tax years 2025–28).
- 1099-NEC threshold rises to $2,000 (tax year 2026).
- SALT cap rises to $40k.
- IRS staff fell about 25%. Direct File was cancelled.
- 2026 filing season: preparer e-files up 0.6%, self-prepared up 1.9%.

**Offshoring.** About 25% of firms offshore work (AICPA, 2023).

## Payroll trends

**OBBBA reporting**
- The final 2026 Form W-2 adds:
  - Box 12 code TP: cash tips that may qualify for the deduction.
  - Box 12 code TT: qualified overtime.
  - Box 12 code TA: Trump account contributions.
  - Box 14b: Treasury tipped-occupation codes.
- Source: "IRS Finalizes 2026 Form W-2," Experian Employer Services, Jan 14, 2026: https://experian.com/blogs/employer-services/irs-finalizes-2026-form-w-2
- 2025 was a transition year. Reporting applies to tax year 2026.
- Overtime must be computed on FLSA definitions even when state law or contracts pay more generously. Tips and overtime remain subject to FICA.
- Source: Daniel Lewis (ADP; president, National Payroll Reporting Consortium) at the PayrollOrg Capital Summit, as reported in "Payroll Leaders Urged to Temper Expectations Around No Tax on Tips and Overtime," Christopher Wood, Thomson Reuters Checkpoint, Mar 17, 2026: https://tax.thomsonreuters.com/news/payroll-leaders-urged-to-temper-expectations-around-no-tax-on-tips-and-overtime-at-capital-summit-opening-session/

**Colorado compliance load**
- FAMLI premium is 0.88% of wages in 2026, down from 0.9% (SB25-144). Source: NFP, Jul 1, 2025: https://www.nfp.com/insights/colorado-enacts-updates-to-co-famli-beginning-2026/
- Not yet researched: state and Denver minimum wage, Colorado SecureSavings.

**Consolidation**
- Paychex bought Paycor for $4.1B all-cash, announced Jan 7, 2025 and closed by Jun 2025. Paychex had about 745k payroll clients; Paycor had 49k+. Source: Constellation Research, Jan 7, 2025: https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/paychex-acquires-paycor-41-billion-deal

**Occupations**
- Payroll and timekeeping clerks: CO 2024–34 −6% (2,660 to 2,510; 260 openings a year). US 2025–35 −16%.
- Not researched: AI in payroll specifically; ADP/Paychex headcount; whether Gusto's Denver office is coded 541214.
